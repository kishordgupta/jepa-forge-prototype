## Interpretation boundaries

- The custom pilot is not official I-JEPA, V-JEPA, or full VICReg. Official models are now evaluated separately as frozen transfer baselines on images and toy videos.
- Source-pixel observation budgets match in transfer; architecture, resizing and external pretraining compute do not. No official attentive-probe benchmark or natural-video benchmark reproduction is claimed.
- The new study fixes initialization to seed 7 and changes the outer split three times. The old study changes initialization three times on one split. Their SDs measure different variability and neither is a population confidence interval.
- Repeated partitions overlap. The original datasets were previously inspected, so repartitioning is not external confirmation. No significance test or broad superiority claim is supported.
- Validation is reused for probe strength and mask/checkpoint choices. There is no nested validation. Test rows/labels are excluded from each selection interface; stateless frozen visual embeddings may be cached without labels before selection.
- Budget parity applies to the fixed/search JEPA pair as optimizer updates and probe-fit counts. It does not mean equal wall time or equal FLOPs across neural architectures, tree models, feature selectors and externally pretrained models.
- Feature selection is supervised through development labels. Generic masks are hypotheses, not causal explanations. Independent coordinate selectors and schema masks have different search spaces despite matching scalar observation counts.
- Sensor forecasting excludes null/transition windows using source labels and uses short horizons. PAMAP2 is decimated without antialias filtering; the selected eight subjects are few. Clinical, unrestricted streaming, deployment and physical-control claims remain unsupported.
- Low covariance rank is a diagnostic, not proof of uselessness or a causal diagnosis. No covariance-penalty ablation or intervention was run.
- All source data, model choices and retained outcomes are disclosed, including unfavorable comparisons. Missing writer/speaker/geographic identifiers in older datasets remain limitations.
